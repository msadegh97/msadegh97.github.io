Hi, I’m Sadegh 👋. I’m a PhD student at the University of Cologne, fortunate to be advised by [Dr. Aleksandar Bojchevski](https://abojchevski.github.io/). I’m currently a research intern at [ElastixAI](https://elastix.ai) in Seattle, working on model compression and fast inference for large language models. Previously, I interned at [Axelera AI](https://axelera.ai), worked as a research assistant at [CISPA](http://cispa.de), and completed my master's degree at [Saarland University](http://saarland-informatics-campus.de).


My current research focuses on trustworthy machine learning and efficient machine learning.
